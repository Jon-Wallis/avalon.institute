What is the Avalon Exposition?

Avalon aims to radically restructure the study of mankind. We believe that in order to study man well, it is necessary to unite many forms of study and expression in a single place.

We are inviting our friends, allies, and everyone who wants to put the life of the mind on a new footing to share their work and art. We aim to create a cornucopia of expression in a free and open format.

The Avalon Exposition is a festival dedicated to the study of mankind.

What is the Avalon Institute?

Avalon is dedicated to creating the intimacy necessary for the collective pursuit of truth.

The Institute continuously experiments with formats that cultivate and communicate passion, building the network and infrastructure needed to found a new home for the revival of art and thought. avalon.institute

Where is the Avalon Exposition?

Lighthaven — a walled campus at 2740 Telegraph Avenue, Berkeley, built for exactly this: stages, salons, gardens, and corners to talk in until late.

Ten minutes from downtown Berkeley BART. Lodging notes and travel details come with your ticket.

Q&A

Where is it happening?

Lighthaven, 2740 Telegraph Avenue, Berkeley — a walled campus built for exactly this kind of weekend.

When does it start and end?

Doors open Friday afternoon, October 30; the exposition closes Sunday evening, November 1. The full schedule arrives closer to the date.

How many people will be there?

Two to three hundred over the course of the weekend.

What does my ticket include?

All three days, Friday afternoon through Sunday night, and the black-tie Halloween evening — confirm your spot for it when you click through. Placeholder: all meals included.

Do I need to bring something to come?

No. An engaged audience is half the exposition. Bring yourself; the rest is optional.

How do I get a slot on the bill?

Say what you're bringing when you get your ticket, or propose it on the wall once you arrive.

Who is this for?

People who take the humanities and the human sciences personally, whatever their day job.

What does a ticket cost?

The early-bird ticket is $350. If cost is the thing standing between you and the weekend, write to us.

Where do I stay?

Lighthaven has a limited number of rooms on site, first come, first served; most guests stay nearby. Placeholder: a lodging list and room block, sent with your ticket.

Can I volunteer?

Placeholder: yes — a few shifts across the weekend in exchange for a reduced ticket. Say so when you register.

What is the refund policy?

Placeholder: full refunds up to seven days before the event.

Can I bring my kids?

Children are welcome with an adult. We don't yet know whether we can offer childcare — ask when you get your ticket and we'll tell you where things stand.

Tickets

Tickets are open: one ticket admits you to all three days of the exposition. It holds two to three hundred people. The black-tie Halloween evening is included — confirm your spot when you get your ticket. If you have something to bring, say so when you get yours.
